Are You Eligible for This Grant?

Review the requirements below or use the short checker for an initial eligibility result.

Core Eligibility Requirements

Active 401(k) Account

You must own a current 401(k) account. The grant is a separate source of funding and does not withdraw from or reduce your 401(k) balance.

Working U.S. Citizen

You must be a U.S. citizen who is currently employed or self-employed.

Business, Personal, or Financial Need

You may request funds for a business purpose, a personal need, or help resolving a financial problem.

U.S. Citizenship

Applicants must be citizens of the United States.

18 Years of Age or Older

Applicants must be 18 or older and able to submit their own information for review.

Valid U.S. Bank Account

Approved applicants need a valid U.S. bank account to receive their grant funds.

How Your Award Amount Is Determined

There is no fixed amount for every applicant. The amount offered to an approved user depends on the review of their submitted information.

01

401(k) Ownership

Your 401(k) confirms eligibility, but the grant is not taken from the account and does not change its balance.

02

Applicant Information

The review team considers the personal, employment, and financial information included in your application.

03

Purpose of the Grant

Explain whether you need support for business expenses, personal needs, or a financial problem.

04

Individual Review

Each application is reviewed individually before approval and the final award amount are decided.

Bonus Benefit

Eligible Users Receive a Credit Card Top-Up

As an added benefit of the program, approved and active participants qualify for a credit card top-up based on account activity, application completeness, and review outcome.

Credit Card Top-Up Benefit

Approved participants · Activity-based tiering

The top-up is applied to the verified credit card on file after your application has been approved and your account activity has been assessed. Top-ups are issued as statement credits or direct-load advances depending on your card issuer.

How Your Tier Is Assigned

Application Completeness
Submitting every required document, a clear ID, and a valid 401(k) statement increases your tier.
401(k) Account Activity
A higher current balance, longer account tenure, and confirmed 401(k) access raise your top-up amount.
Card Verification & Usage
A verified card with a valid billing ZIP, legible card images, and a positive account standing supports the maximum tier.
